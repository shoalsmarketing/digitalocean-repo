Three things in one purchase

Most coverage of this deal focuses on the 50% discount. That is the headline, but the offer actually bundles three pieces:

  1. WebinarJam on Basic, Professional or Enterprise at half the regular first-year annual price.
  2. EverWebinar, for automated webinars, included with the qualifying plan.
  3. The Webinar Launch Accelerator kit, valued at $1,841, to help you build the presentation and script.

Each one is worth understanding before you buy, because they solve different problems.

EverWebinar: included, as it normally is

EverWebinar is the automated side of the platform. You record a webinar once and schedule it to play for new registrants, instead of presenting live every time.

To be clear about the value: EverWebinar is normally included in Basic, Professional and Enterprise. The promotion does not add it as an extra; it comes with the qualifying plan as usual.

That still matters when comparing plans. Starter, which is excluded from the deal, does not include automated webinars at all.

The Webinar Launch Accelerator kit

The bonus is a kit valued at $1,841 that helps you prepare your webinar presentation and script. For first-time hosts, building the presentation is often the slowest part of getting a webinar live, so this is aimed at that step.

The kit includes an AI Webinar Script Wizard. It needs a separate ChatGPT Plus subscription, which is not included in the offer. If you do not have ChatGPT Plus, factor that into your plans or use the rest of the kit without the wizard.

The $1,841 figure is the stated value of the kit, not a cash discount. Judge it by whether you need help building the presentation.

Who can buy it

Eligibility is broader than many holiday software deals. The promotion is open to:

  • New customers
  • Former customers
  • Trial users

It is not open to Starter plans or to accounts on legacy pricing.

If you already have an account

Existing customers should email payments@genesisdigital.co to check eligibility before the deadline. That includes anyone on a monthly plan who wants to upgrade to annual at the promotional price.

Send that email early in the window. The promotion closes Thursday, December 3 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific, and support replies take time.

Who might skip the bundle

The offer is strong for anyone who runs webinars regularly, but it is not for everyone.

If you host only small live sessions, under 100 people and under an hour, a regular Starter year at $468 covers that. Just know that discounted Basic costs only $6 more at $474 and adds 500 attendees, 2 hosts, 2-hour sessions and automated webinars.

If you are on a legacy-priced account, you are excluded, so there is nothing to claim. And if your plan is to rely heavily on the AI Script Wizard, budget for ChatGPT Plus separately.

Finally, remember the renewal. The discount covers one annual term. Basic renews at $948, Professional at $2,748 and Enterprise at $4,548 unless you cancel.

How to claim it

Choose the plan, pay for the year up front, and keep the confirmation. WebinarJam lists a 30-day money-back guarantee.
